The Concept

Two days, one story

We bring together people from FINN, Oikotie, and Vend for two days that connect our proud history with the future we need to build.

Day 1 at Thorbjørnrud grounds us in who we are. Day 2 takes us to Kistefos Museum — where contemporary art becomes a lens for thinking differently about innovation.

Day 1 · Thorbjørnrud

State of the union, our journey, real impact, and external perspectives. Ends with art, innovation, and cross-team connection.

Day 2 · Kistefos Museum

Art as inspiration. Groups explore the museum and create a digital artwork using AI — one vision per team.

The Digital Canvas

10 group artworks assembled live into a single mosaic — our collective vision for the future of Vend RE.

Group Exercise

The Digital Canvas

Split into 10 groups. Each group gets a disruption theme — a "lens." At Kistefos, they find art that inspires them, then use AI image generation to create one digital artwork. All 10 pieces are assembled live into a single mosaic — our collective vision.

Each group delivers
  • One AI-generated artwork
  • A title for the piece
  • A one-sentence artist statement
  • A 2–3 minute presentation
