The Concept

Two days, one story

We bring together 150 people from FINN, Oikotie, and Vend for two days that connect our proud history with the future we need to build. Day 1 grounds us in who we are and the forces reshaping our world. Day 2 takes us to Kistefos Museum — where contemporary art becomes a lens for thinking differently about innovation. The gathering ends with a collaborative artwork created by all of us — The Digital Canvas.

Day 1 · Oslo Our history, our impact, and the forces of change. Ends with a provocation we carry to Day 2.
Day 2 · Kistefos Museum Art as inspiration. Groups explore the museum and create a digital artwork using AI — one vision per team.
The Digital Canvas 10 group artworks assembled live into a single mosaic — our collective vision for the future of Vend RE.

Group Exercise

The Digital Canvas

150 people → 10 groups of 15. Each group gets a disruption theme from Day 1. At Kistefos, they find art that inspires them, then use AI image generation to create one digital artwork. All 10 pieces are assembled live into a single mosaic — our collective vision.
Each group delivers
  • One AI-generated artwork
  • A title for the piece
  • A one-sentence artist statement
  • A 2–3 minute presentation
