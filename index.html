The Concept

The Art of Next

What happens when art meets strategy? We are bringing together everyone in Vend Real Estate — FINN, Oikotie, and the Vend team — to explore how creative thinking can shape the future of our industry.

Day 1 grounds us in who we are and where we're headed. Day 2 takes us to Kistefos Museum, where contemporary art becomes a lens for seeing innovation differently.

Individual Activity

The Selfie Mosaic

During the museum tour, every person takes one selfie in front of an artwork that inspires them. All ~150 photos are assembled into a single mosaic — a portrait of us through the art we chose.

The mosaic is revealed during the closing session and becomes a recurring visual at future meetings: this is who we are, and this is how we see.

Submit via a shared link or QR code → automatic assembly

Mockup: ~150 selfies assembled into one image
Group Exercise

The Digital Canvas

10 groups. 10 disruption lenses. 10 AI-generated artworks. One collective vision.

Each group delivers
  • One AI-generated artwork
  • A title for the piece
  • A one-sentence artist statement
  • A 2–3 minute presentation

All 10 pieces are assembled live during the closing into a single mosaic — inspired by Beeple's "Everydays" concept. The result becomes our lasting artifact.

10 Disruption Lenses
  • 01 AI & Home Search
  • 02 Trust & Data Transparency
  • 03 New Competitors & Marketplace Dynamics
  • 04 Sustainability & Green Housing
  • 05 The Agent of the Future
  • 06 Global Shifts & New Market Models
  • 07 Generational Change in Homebuying
  • 08 Beyond Listings — Platform Innovation
  • 09 The Human Element in a Digital World
  • 10 Regulation as Innovation Driver
